Finance, in everyday language

Call
An option that benefits from a sufficiently large upward move. The move must happen before it expires.
Put
An option that benefits from a sufficiently large downward move, before it expires.
Volatility
How much a stock moves around. More movement does not automatically make an option a good deal.
Liquidity
The ability to get cash or trade without a big price penalty. Company cash problems and market trading problems are different.
Catalyst
An event that could change the story, such as earnings or a policy announcement.
Time decay
An option can lose value as time passes, even if your eventual direction is right.
M / B
M means million; B means billion. Tap company details for the source figures.
Implied volatility
How much movement is already built into the option’s price. Paying too much can ruin a correct idea.
